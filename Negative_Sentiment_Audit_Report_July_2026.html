Top Reasons Customers Are Dissatisfied
1
Delivery Delayed Logistics
Orders not dispatched within TAT, physical delivery slots missed, no proactive delay alerts sent to cx. Makes up 10.5% of all cases, a touch below June's 11.2% share — still the single biggest driver, but not growing faster than overall volume.

Not Happy with Policy Customer / Policy
Customers pushing back on a policy they were told about, mostly LTE/LTB values and PoP refund terms. Now 8.1% of all cases, a genuine increase from just 0.7% of cases in June.

Post Sale Whatsapp has the most repeat customers (92), followed by Post Sale Inbound (77) and WhatsApp (55). Improving first-contact resolution on delivery queries remains the single biggest lever to reduce repeat cx volume.
